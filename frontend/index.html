    How scoring works
    • One point for every correct Home / Draw / Away pick.
    • Cup ties decided on penalties count as a win for the side that went through.
    • Rounds marked Final are published by the organizer in the sheet. Scores for the round in play come from live results and may change until the organizer confirms them.
    • Ranks are shared on equal points.
